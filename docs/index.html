What this page assumes

    Shortfall does arithmetic on the hours and the rules you select. It is not legal advice, it does not read images, and it cannot know what your contract says. Where the law depends on a fact the app cannot see — workplace size, contract type, a rest break actually taken — it asks instead of assuming.
